**Thanks for testing Label Hero!**

Label Hero is two music careers in one game: manage a band through ten years, or run a record label for thirty. This is an early build, so expect rough edges. Your feedback shapes what comes next.

**Please try:**

1. **Start a band.** Play at least one full in-game year (12 months) until the Year in Review appears in December. Plan gigs, write and record songs, release something, and try a tour.
2. **Start a label.** Pick 1975, 2000 or 2025. Sign an artist from Scouting, record and release music, and play through to the first December: the month report, the Golden Radio Awards and the year recap.
3. **Use more than one save slot.** Then close the app completely, reopen it, and check that your progress is still there.
4. **Open Settings** from the title screen and try the sound, vibration and tips switches.
5. **On an iPad,** rotate between portrait and landscape on a few screens.

**Things I'd love to hear about:**

- Anything that crashes, freezes or loses your progress
- Text that's cut off, overlapping or hard to read
- Moments where you weren't sure what to do next
- Balance: did your band go broke too fast? Did your label get rich too easily? Did any choice feel pointless?
- What made you want to play "one more month", and what made you stop

**How to send feedback:** take a screenshot in the app and TestFlight will offer to send it with a note. You can also use "Send Beta Feedback" in the TestFlight app. Please mention your device and which mode you were playing.

**Known limits in this build:**

- No cloud sync. Saves stay on your device, and deleting the app deletes them.
- Numbers and balance are still being tuned.

All bands, artists, labels, festivals and events in the game are fictional.
