# Label Hero Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 6, 2026

Label Hero is a music-management game for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it.

## The short version

Label Hero doesn't collect any data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

## What the app stores

Your saved games, your band career progress and your settings (sound, vibration and tips) are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

- You can delete all games and progress at any time in **Settings › Delete all games and progress**.
- Deleting the app also deletes everything it has stored.

## What the app does not do

- It does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, contacts or location.
- It does not use analytics, crash-reporting services or advertising networks.
- It does not track you across other apps or websites.
- It does not share or sell any data, because it doesn't collect any.

## TestFlight beta testing

If you test a beta version through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may share some information with the developer: your name and email address if you were invited by email, crash reports, and any feedback or screenshots you choose to send. Apple's privacy policy covers this information, which is used only to fix problems and improve the game. See [Apple's TestFlight privacy information](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/test-flight/).

## Children

Label Hero doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version of Label Hero changes how it handles data, for example by adding ads, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
